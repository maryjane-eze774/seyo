Yeza

Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

The short version. Yeza is where you share your life and your content: live Real moments, uploads from your camera roll, stories and videos. For moments tied to a place, we use the place you post from. We don't sell your data, we don't use your personal data for targeted ads, and you can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time. Background location is off unless you turn on Nearby alerts, and you can turn it off again whenever you like.

Who we are

Yeza is a social app run by Chinenye Eze in the United Kingdom ("Yeza", "we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal data described here. You can reach us at yezasupport@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, username and password (stored encrypted by our login provider).
  • Sign in with Apple or Google: if you choose one of these, we receive your name, your email address (or Apple's private relay address if you choose to hide it) and an account identifier. We don't receive your Apple or Google password, contacts or anything else from those accounts.
  • Profile: your display name, bio and profile photo, if you add them.
  • What you post: photos and videos you capture in the app or upload from your camera roll, stories, captions, hashtags, comments, sparks and messages you send. If you edit an upload (crop, filters, brightness, trim, cover frame), we save the edited version.
  • Location while you use the app: when you capture a Real moment, or choose to add a place to an upload, we save the location and the place name (for example "Grafton Street, Hull"). Uploads without a place don't save a location. We also use your location while the app is open to show what's near you, tell you when you're at a live place, and check drops you claim are claimed in person.
  • Location in the background (only if you turn on Nearby alerts): see the Nearby alerts section below.
  • Your activity: who you follow, who follows you, events you join, drops you claim, daily challenges you enter and vote in, trips and places you save, people you block and things you report.
  • Device information: a notification token if you turn on notifications, and basic technical data (device type, app version, error logs) so we can keep the app working.

Nearby alerts and background location

Nearby alerts are off by default. If you turn them on in You → Settings → Nearby alerts and allow location "Always" on your phone:

  • Your phone watches for when you arrive at places near you that are popular on Yeza, using your phone's built-in location features, even when Yeza is closed.
  • When you arrive, your phone shows you a notification, at most once per place per day. The alert is created on your phone.
  • From time to time your phone sends your approximate location to our server only to fetch the list of places around you. We don't store this background location, we don't build a history of where you've been, and we never show it to anyone.
  • You can turn Nearby alerts off at any time in Settings, or by changing Yeza's location permission in your phone's settings. Logging out or deleting your account also turns them off.

Who can see what

  • Your profile and moments are public to other Yeza users, including the place name they were posted from.
  • Your exact location is never shown to other people as a live dot. Others see the place a moment was posted from, not where you are now.
  • "You were both there" shows two people that they posted from the same place. It only uses moments you have already made public.
  • Live events spotted by Yeza: when many people post from the same spot at the same time, Yeza may show it as a live event, named from a popular hashtag. It is built only from moments that are already public, and it shows how many people are there, not who.
  • Yeza Now entries and results are shown on your profile. Who you voted for is never shown to anyone.
  • Messages are only visible to the people in the conversation.
  • People you block can't see your moments, stories or comments, and can't message you.

Why we use your data

  • To run Yeza: show your moments and content to others, build Rooms, Happenings, city pages and the map, run the daily Yeza Now challenge, and let you follow and message people (this is needed to provide the service you signed up for).
  • To keep Yeza safe: review reports, remove content that breaks our community guidelines, and stop spam and abuse (our legitimate interest in a safe community).
  • To send notifications and Nearby alerts you've turned on (your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in the app or your phone settings).
  • To fix bugs and improve the app (our legitimate interest).

Events from other websites

Some events in Yeza are public listings from Ticketmaster. We don't send Ticketmaster any of your personal data. If you tap Get tickets, you leave Yeza and go to Ticketmaster's website, where their own terms and privacy policy apply. Yeza may earn a small commission if you buy tickets there; this never changes the price you pay.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal data, we don't share it with advertisers, and we don't use it to target ads at you. If Yeza shows promoted events, places or drops, they are clearly labelled and chosen by location and time, not by tracking you. We only use service providers who help us run Yeza:

  • Supabase hosts our database, login and file storage.
  • Apple and Google, if you choose to sign in with them, and for delivering push notifications and showing maps.
  • Expo, which helps deliver app updates and notifications.

Some of these providers may store data outside the UK. When they do, we rely on the legal protections the law requires, such as the UK's approved data transfer terms.

We may also share data if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. Stories disappear after 24 hours. Background location used for Nearby alerts is not stored. When you delete your account, your profile, moments, stories, comments, sparks, follows, saved trips and messages you sent are deleted from our systems. Backups are cleared within 30 days.

Deleting your account

Open Yeza, go to You → Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything in it. You can also email us and we'll do it for you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under UK GDPR and Nigeria's Data Protection Act), you can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, get a copy of it, or object to how we use it. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Email yezasupport@gmail.com and we'll reply within 30 days. In the UK you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Age

You must be 18 or older to use Yeza. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think a child is using Yeza, please tell us and we'll remove the account.

Security

Your data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with access rules so people can only see what they're allowed to. No system is perfect, so please use a strong password.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and tell you in the app if the change is important.
