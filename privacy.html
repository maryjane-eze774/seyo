Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

The short version. Seyo is where you share your life and your content: live Real moments, uploads from your camera roll, stories and videos. For moments tied to a place, we use the place you post from. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and you can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

Seyo is a social app run by Chinenye Eze in the United Kingdom ("Seyo", "we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal data described here. You can reach us at seyo.support@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, username, password (stored encrypted by our login provider), and, if you use them, your Apple or Google sign-in identifier.
  • Profile: your display name, bio and profile photo, if you add them.
  • What you post: photos and videos you capture in the app or upload from your camera roll, stories, captions, hashtags, comments, sparks and messages you send.
  • Location: when you capture a Real moment, or choose to add a place to an upload, we save the location and the place name (for example "Grafton Street, Hull"). Uploads without a place don't save a location. We also use your location while the app is open to show what's near you, tell you when you're at a live place, and check drops you claim are claimed in person. We do not track your location in the background when the app is closed.
  • Your activity: who you follow, who follows you, events you join, drops you claim, people you block and things you report.
  • Device information: a notification token if you turn on notifications, and basic technical data (device type, app version, error logs) so we can keep the app working.

Who can see what

  • Your profile and moments are public to other Seyo users, including the place name they were posted from.
  • Your exact location is never shown to other people as a live dot. Others see the place a moment was posted from, not where you are now.
  • "You were both there" shows two people that they posted from the same place. It only uses moments you have already made public.
  • Messages are only visible to the people in the conversation.
  • People you block can't see your moments, stories or comments, and can't message you.

Why we use your data

  • To run Seyo: show your moments and content to others, build Rooms, Happenings and the map, and let you follow and message people (this is needed to provide the service you signed up for).
  • To keep Seyo safe: review reports, remove content that breaks our community guidelines, and stop spam and abuse (our legitimate interest in a safe community).
  • To send notifications you've turned on (your consent, which you can withdraw in your phone settings).
  • To fix bugs and improve the app (our legitimate interest).

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal data and we don't share it with advertisers. We only use service providers who help us run Seyo:

  • Supabase hosts our database, login and file storage.
  • Apple and Google, if you choose to sign in with them, and for delivering push notifications.
  • Expo, which helps deliver app updates and notifications.

We may also share data if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. Stories disappear after 24 hours. When you delete your account, your profile, moments, stories, comments, sparks, follows and messages you sent are deleted from our systems. Backups are cleared within 30 days.

Deleting your account

Open Seyo, go to You → Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything in it. You can also email us and we'll do it for you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under UK GDPR and Nigeria's Data Protection Act), you can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, get a copy of it, or object to how we use it. Email seyo.support@gmail.com and we'll reply within 30 days. In the UK you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Age

You must be 18 or older to use Seyo. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think a child is using Seyo, please tell us and we'll remove the account.

Security

Your data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with access rules so people can only see what they're allowed to. No system is perfect, so please use a strong password.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and tell you in the app if the change is important.
