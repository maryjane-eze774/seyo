Community guidelines

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Seyo is for real people sharing their lives: live Real moments, uploads, stories and creator content. Be yourself, be kind, and respect the people and places around you. Breaking these rules can get your content removed or your account suspended.

Keep it real

  • Real moments must be captured live in the app at the place you say. Don't try to fake a Real badge or a location.
  • Don't pretend to be someone else, or a business or organisation you don't represent.
  • One person, one account. No fake accounts, bots or bought followers.
  • Uploads are welcome. Just don't label an upload as Real, or tag it at a place, if it wasn't taken there.

Creators

  • Only post content you made or have the right to share. Credit people you collaborate with.
  • If you're paid or given something for free to post about a brand, say so clearly (for example #ad or "paid partnership").
  • Don't mislead people with fake giveaways, edited "results" sold as real, or clickbait that hides what a post is.

Respect people

  • No bullying, harassment, threats or hate against anyone, including because of race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or illness.
  • Don't post private information about other people, such as their home address or phone number.
  • Ask before you post close-ups of people who clearly don't want to be filmed. If someone asks you to take a moment down, please do.
  • Never post intimate images of anyone without their consent.

Keep it safe

  • No nudity or sexual content.
  • Nothing that sexualises or endangers children, ever. We report this to the authorities.
  • No graphic violence, and nothing that encourages people to hurt themselves or others.
  • No selling or promoting illegal drugs, weapons or other illegal goods.
  • Don't encourage dangerous or reckless behaviour, including drink-driving.

Events and drops

  • Hosts must give honest details about events and drops, and honour drops that people claim.
  • Drops involving alcohol are for people 18 and over, and must follow local laws.
  • No scams, fake tickets or misleading offers.

No spam

  • Don't flood feeds, Rooms or messages with repeated posts, links or ads.
  • Only post content you have the right to share. Don't repost other people's work as your own.

Reporting and blocking

If something breaks these rules, tap ⋯ on the moment, comment or profile and choose Report. We review reports within 24 hours and remove content that breaks these guidelines. You can also Block anyone: they won't be able to see your moments or message you. You can manage blocked people in Settings → Blocked accounts.

For anything urgent, email maryjaneflora2@gmail.com. If someone is in danger, contact your local emergency services first.

What happens if rules are broken

Depending on how serious it is, we may remove the content, limit your account, or remove it permanently. If you think we got it wrong, email us and we'll take another look.
